About FinSecure

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Why FinSecure exists

Fraud and identity theft cost Americans tens of billions of dollars every year, and most of it is preventable with the right habits. But most "security advice" online is either fear-mongering or a sales pitch.

FinSecure publishes clear, practical, human-written guides to protecting your money: how scams actually work, the exact red flags, and what to do when things go wrong.

Who writes the guides

Content is researched and written by independent writers with a background in technology and consumer protection, and reviewed for accuracy before publication. No article is written or published by AI without human editorial review — and every claim about products, services, or statistics is sourced where possible.

Our editorial promise

  • Independence: affiliate relationships never change our recommendations or rankings.
  • Accuracy: we cite sources for statistics and product claims.
  • Honesty: when we don't know, we say so. When a tool isn't worth it, we say that too.
  • Currency: financial threats change fast; we update guides regularly and show the update date.
