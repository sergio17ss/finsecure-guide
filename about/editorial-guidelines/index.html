Editorial Guidelines & Sources

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Research and sourcing

Articles rely on authoritative sources including: U.S. FTC and CFPB publications, FBI/IC3 announcements, state attorney general warnings, peer-reviewed security research, vendor documentation, and primary court or agency records. We link sources inline whenever they support a specific fact or statistic.

Human review process

Every draft is reviewed by an editor before publication, with a checklist that includes: accuracy of statistics, absence of dangerous or illegal advice, clear red-flag guidance, and plain-language readability. We never publish content promoting hacking, malware, or illegal tools.

Updates and corrections

Financial threats evolve. We review high-traffic and time-sensitive guides quarterly and update them, always showing the update date on the page. If a reader identifies an error, we investigate and correct it promptly, and note significant corrections at the bottom of the article.

Our lines in the sand

  • No investment advice or buy/sell recommendations for specific securities.
  • No promises of returns, "get rich quick," or financial guarantee wording.
  • No offensive security content (malware, exploits, how to hack).
  • No undisclosed affiliate links — every affiliate relationship is disclosed.
